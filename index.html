Theoretical condensed matter physics

Zhengyan Darius Shi

Postdoctoral Fellow · Stanford University

I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist at the Leinweber Institute for Theoretical Physics at Stanford University.

My research explores how strong interactions give rise to new phases of quantum matter. I am particularly interested in topological phases, unconventional superconductivity, non-Fermi liquids, and quantum criticality.
